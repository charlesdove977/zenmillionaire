## Money IQ vs Money EQ
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Financial intelligence (saving, investing, tax) is only half. Money EQ, the emotional relationship with money, is equally important. High-IQ / low-EQ people (athletes, stars) earn fortunes and lose them because the emotional side is untrained.

> "Money emotional intelligence is as important as money IQ, financial intelligence." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Train both hands; when a money decision feels charged, ask if IQ or EQ is driving.

## The Money Blueprint (inherited)
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Beliefs about money are installed by parents, who inherited them from grandparents, three generations of wounds. You cannot change money behavior until you rewrite the belief underneath it.

> "Unless we change what we believe, we cannot change what we're going to do." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Name one money rule you inherited ("money doesn't grow on trees"); decide consciously whether to keep it.

## Happy Money vs Unhappy Money
[Source: Ken Honda video]

The same dollar carries different energy. Happy money makes you smile to receive/spend it and is earned doing work you love. Unhappy money carries guilt, shame, or resentment, earned unethically or doing work you hate ("money crying in your wallet").

> "Happy money is money that makes you smile when you receive it." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Audit your income: is it happy or unhappy money? Shift the source if it disturbs your peace.

## The Thank-You Protocol
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Say thank you to money as it arrives AND as it leaves. Appreciating the flow creates a bigger flow. His A/B test: clients he thanked + gave small gifts sent far more referrals.

> "Thank you money for being with me. Even though it was a short visit, you can come back anytime." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** For one week, thank money on every inflow and outflow. Watch the relationship shift.

## The Seven Money Personalities
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Spender, Saver, Money-Maker, Gambler, Warrior/Worrier, Indifferent, Savior. No type is better; opposites attract then clash (the trait that drew you together becomes the divorce reason). Awareness, not manipulation, resolves it.

> "I don't judge people. I'm not saying which type is better than the other." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Identify your top type and your partner's; use it for an open dialogue, not to "fix" them.

## The Money Container & "More Hell"
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Everyone has a money-container size. The West asks "how do I grow it?"; Japan asks "how do I be content with what I have?" Chasing endless more is "more hell."

> "If you're stuck in getting more, giving more, earning more, then you're in a hell of more." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Define your "enough" number before scaling. Growth from contentment, not from lack.

## The 70/70 = 100% Mechanic
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Set the bar at 70 and hit 70, you are 100% satisfied. Grow FROM contentment rather than toward it. Swap the "motivation/drive" engine (often dark fuel) for a cleaner one; you still grow, but happy the whole way.

> "70 out of 70, you're 100%." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Lower an impossible bar to one you can fully meet; let overflow, not pressure, drive growth.

## Billionaires vs Millionaires
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Billionaires can't calculate risk, they see only possibility, go all-in, and endure multiple bankruptcies. Too much money endangers family and peace. Enough beats maximum.

> "Billionaires don't have the capability of thinking of the risk. They always think of the possibility." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Don't copy billionaire risk behavior; aim for "enough for freedom," not maximum.

## Do What You Love, Make Money Follow (ikigai / dharma)
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Pursue what you love but ENSURE money follows it. Some ikigai/dharma doesn't pay, so fund purpose from other income. Don't assume more purpose = more money.

> "Do what you love and make sure money follows you." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Separate life-purpose from income; if your purpose doesn't pay, build a separate money system for it.

## Money From the Past (residual systems)
[Source: Ken Honda video]

Wealthy people get paid from past work, royalties, books reprinting, real estate. "Working hard" isn't the lever; building a system that pays you later is.

> "Wealthy people, they get paid from the past." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** Build one asset now (content, product, royalty) that pays you repeatedly later.

## Healing Crisis & the 14 True Wealths
[Source: Ken Honda video]

When everything collapses, it's a reset, welcome it. Money is only 1 of 14 forms of true wealth (peace of mind, friendship, health, trust, experiences, love...). The strongest insurance is community and friends, not hoarded cash.

> "True wealth comes from the ability to create wealth." (Ken Honda video)

**Apply:** In crisis, inventory your other wealths; lean on community. Rebuild from there.
