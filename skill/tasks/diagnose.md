# Task: Diagnose (money blueprint + personality)

Purpose: build a short profile of the user's relationship with money so every
future session personalizes. Grounded in Ken Honda's blueprint + 7-personality
work and Housel's "no one's crazy."

## Run as a survey (1/2/3 picks + open follow-ups)
Use AskUserQuestion for the pick-one items. Keep it to ~6-8 questions total.

1. **Money blueprint (inherited).** Open: "Growing up, what did you hear about
   money at home?" Then pick-one: was money treated as scarce / neutral /
   abundant? (Honda video: 3-generation blueprint)
2. **Dominant personality type** (Honda's seven): Spender, Saver, Money-Maker,
   Gambler, Warrior/Worrier, Indifferent, Savior. Ask 2-3 behavior questions,
   infer the top 1-2, confirm with the user.
3. **Happy vs unhappy money:** does money currently come from work they love or
   dread? (Honda video)
4. **More vs enough:** where are they on the "more hell" ↔ "70/70 = 100%"
   spectrum? (Honda video; Housel "never enough")
5. **Offense vs defense:** better at earning or at keeping? (Millionaire Next
   Door: PAW/UAW)
6. **Goal + constraint:** what do they actually want money FOR, and what's the
   real constraint (income, spending, belief, fear)?

## Output
Write/update a profile the other tasks can read. Store at:
`sources/transcripts/USER-PROFILE.md` (front matter: blueprint, top types,
happy/unhappy, more-vs-enough, offense/defense, goal, constraint, dated).
Confirm the profile back to the user in 5-6 lines. Cite the frameworks each
reading rests on. Then offer to route into advise or council.

## Rule
Diagnosis is a mirror, not a verdict. No judgment of types (Honda: "I don't
judge people"). Reflect, confirm, personalize.
