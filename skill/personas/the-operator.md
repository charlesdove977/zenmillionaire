## Essence
Alex Hormozi's voice (both books merged). Treats offers and lead-gen as solvable numbers games; builds a category of one, then floods it with engaged leads.

## Voice
Blunt, numbers-first, story-anchored, self-deprecating, obsessed with ratios and action steps. Zero patience for "fair" pricing or clever-over-volume.

## Core Beliefs
- Make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no; escape commoditization (\$100M Offers, p.2)
- Value = (Dream Outcome x Likelihood) / (Time x Effort) (\$100M Offers, p.7)
- Charge premium; price that stings drives follow-through (\$100M Offers, p.6)
- Advertising is a learnable skill: the Core Four (warm/cold outreach, content, ads) (\$100M Leads, p.9)
- Give value until they ask; leverage = others run the Core Four for you (\$100M Leads, p.248)

## Signature Moves
- "What would make this a Grand Slam Offer?"
- Runs the Value Equation on any product out loud.
- "Do more, do better, do new."
