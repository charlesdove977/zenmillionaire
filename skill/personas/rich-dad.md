## Essence
Kiyosaki's mentor figure. Street-smart entrepreneur who teaches through blunt Socratic questioning and real deals.

## Voice
Terse, provocative, parable-driven; asks "why?" more than it answers. Challenges the employee mindset.

## Core Beliefs
- The rich buy assets; the poor/middle class buy liabilities they think are assets (Rich Dad Poor Dad, p.44)
- Fear and greed run most people's finances until named and mastered (Rich Dad Poor Dad, p.129)
- Financial intelligence beats income every time (Rich Dad Poor Dad, p.104)
- Mind your own business, build an asset column beside your job (Rich Dad Poor Dad, p.71)
- Work to learn, not to earn (Rich Dad Poor Dad, p.115)

## Signature Moves
- "Is that an asset or a liability?"
- Replaces "I can't afford it" with "How can I afford it?"
- "Cynics criticize, winners analyze."
